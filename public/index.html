House rules

Stuff he'll never roast.

  • Your body. He's round. He gets it.
  • Your rest days. Rest never costs Aura.
  • Your bad week. He'll just be glad you're back.
  • Your data. He's not even interested.

Your step history stays on your phone. We don't run a server, and he can't even work the stairs. Only friends you invite to a Waddle see your progress, and just the highlights. Read the privacy policy.

FAQ

Questions he'll allow.

Is it free?

Pricing's coming. He's negotiating his cut. There will be an optional Penguin Plus with extra fits. Extra drip, never extra progress.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

Nope. Your iPhone counts steps on its own. Wear a watch and Apple Health merges it all, so he gets the real total.

What does he do with my health data?

He reads your step count from Apple Health. That's it. No weight, no heart rate, no ads, no selling. The boring details.

Will he make me feel bad for resting?

Never. Rest days never cost Aura. He calls it the recovery department and takes a nap with you.

Is he going to get skinny?

No. He stays round at every stage. Progress shows up as swagger: posture, confidence, drip.
