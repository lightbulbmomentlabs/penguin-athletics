Privacy Policy

Short version: your steps stay on your phone. He just reads them.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Who we are

The Penguin app (“the app”) and this website are published by Lightbulb Moment Labs, Inc. (“we,” “us”). Questions about this policy: gary@penguinathletics.com.

The short version

  • The app reads your step count from Apple Health. Your step count and step history stay on your device.
  • A few features send a small amount of information off your phone, and only what's listed below:
    • Usage stats (on unless you turn them off): which features you use, to PostHog. Never your steps or health data.
    • Gary's fresh lines (only if you say yes): rough goal progress, to Anthropic's AI.
    • The Rookery (only if you join): your penguin's name and look, on our server.
    • Waddles (only if you create or join one): a progress summary, through Apple's iCloud.
    • Weather in his sky (only if you turn it on): your approximate location, to Apple Weather only.
  • No accounts, no ads, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

What the app uses, and why

Step count from Apple Health

With your permission, the app reads your step count from Apple Health (HealthKit). It doesn't read any other health data. Your steps power the game: your goal, the penguin's mood and form, Aura, streaks, unlocks, expedition progress, and widgets.

Your step count and history stay on your device. They are never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never sent to analytics, and never shared with anyone. The only things worked out from your steps that can leave your phone are rough goal progress for Gary's lines (unless you turn them off) and the waddle summary (if you join one), both described below.

Saving walks to Apple Health

If you start a walk in the app and allow it, the walk is saved to Apple Health as a walking workout, with its distance and active energy, so it shows up in Fitness. This is written to Health on your device. We don't receive it.

Motion & Fitness

During a walk, and to keep your count fresh between Health updates, the app reads your iPhone's step count from its motion sensors (Core Motion). This stays on your device.

Usage stats (PostHog)

To learn which features people use and where the app gets confusing, the app sends usage events to PostHog, an analytics service that processes the data for us. An event is something like “finished setup,” “opened the Locker,” “started a walk,” or “started a free trial.” Each event includes:

  • a random analytics ID the app creates (not your name, email, Apple ID, or your device's advertising ID);
  • the app version, iOS version, device model, and language;
  • when it happened;
  • your rough location (country and region), which PostHog works out from your IP address. We've set PostHog not to store the IP address itself.

Usage stats never include your step count, step history, or any other Apple Health data, your penguin's name, waddle members or pokes, or your location beyond country and region. We don't record your screen. We use usage stats only to improve the app: not for advertising, not combined with data from other companies, and never sold. You can turn them off anytime in Settings › Share usage stats. See PostHog's privacy policy for how PostHog handles data.

Gary's fresh lines (only if you say yes)

The app asks you once, after you set up your penguin, and nothing is sent unless you say yes. With Fresh lines from AI on, some of Gary's speech bubbles and notifications are written by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. To write a line, the app sends our line server:

  • your penguin's name, tone setting, and outfit;
  • roughly how close you are to today's goal, to the nearest 10% (never your step count);
  • your streak, and whether today is a rest day;
  • the time of day and day of week;
  • where you are on your expedition;
  • the penguin names and rough goal progress of up to three waddle friends;
  • the last few lines he said, so he doesn't repeat himself;
  • if Weather in his sky is on, the kind of weather where you are (like “rain”) and whether it's very hot or cold. Never your location.

Our server passes this to Anthropic to write the line and doesn't store it. Anthropic doesn't use it to train its models, and may keep it for a limited time for safety under its own policies. You can turn this off anytime in Settings › Fresh lines from AI. Gary then uses only his hand-written lines.

The Rookery (optional)

The Rookery shows every penguin that has joined on one shared sheet of ice. If you join, our server stores:

  • your penguin's name, body, and outfit;
  • when it last saw you walk (a date, not your steps);
  • the stickers your penguin has found;
  • roasts and cheers between penguins (who sent which kind to whom, and when), kept for 7 days;
  • a random ID for your penguin, made from a secret kept in your iCloud Keychain so a new phone keeps the same penguin.

When you walk your penguin around the ice with the joystick, other people with the Rookery open see it move in real time. To do that, the app sends our server your penguin's spot on the ice (not your real location), the way it's facing, and how fast it's walking, a few times a second, only while the Rookery is open. The server passes it on and doesn't keep it: it's gone when you close the Rookery. Others can tell from this that you're in the app right now.

Everyone using the app can see your penguin's name, body, and outfit. Names go through a filter, and some are checked by Anthropic's AI, before they're shown. Names that don't pass show as a number instead.

If you don't walk for 7 days, your penguin is hidden until you're back. Penguins that haven't been seen for 90 days are deleted. You can leave anytime in Settings, which deletes your penguin from our server.

If you report a penguin, we store the report (which penguin, when, and a random ID for your install) for up to 60 days so we can review it.

Weather in his sky (optional, off until you turn it on)

If you turn on Weather in his sky, the app uses your device's approximate location (about city level; it asks for Precise Location off) to get the current weather from Apple Weather (WeatherKit), only while the app is open. Rain, snow, or sunshine then shows up in the app and its widgets. Your location goes only to Apple to look up the weather. We never receive it, and the app doesn't store it.

The app keeps only the kind of sky (for example “rain”) and whether it's very hot or cold, for a few hours. With Fresh lines from AI on, those two words can go to our line server so Gary can talk about the weather. Never your location or the exact temperature. Turn it off anytime in Settings › Weather in his sky, or turn off location for the app in iOS Settings. Weather data is provided by Apple Weather; see Settings in the app for its data sources.

Things you enter and progress the app works out

Your penguin's name, goal, tone, rest days, Aura, streaks, unlocks, expedition progress, and history are stored on your device, including in storage shared with the app's widgets. They may be included in your device backups, depending on your settings.

Waddles (optional)

Waddles let you see how friends are doing. When you create or join a waddle, the app stores the following in iCloud using Apple's CloudKit, where the waddle's members can see it:

  • an ID iCloud assigns to your account for this app (not your name or email);
  • your penguin's name, Aura, form (stage), and outfit, and its Rookery ID if you've joined the Rookery;
  • today's progress toward your goal, rounded to the nearest 10%, plus your streak and whether today is a rest day;
  • when your summary was last updated;
  • the waddle's name;
  • pokes: who sent them, who they were for, the message, and when.

Your step counts and step history are not shared. Waddle data lives in the iCloud accounts of the waddle's owner and members, not on our servers, and we can't read it. iCloud sharing is provided by Apple and is also covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. Depending on your iCloud settings, Apple may show your iCloud name to other members.

Anyone with your waddle's invite link can join, so share it only with people you want in. The owner can remove members or close the waddle, which deletes it for everyone. You can report or block a member from their menu in the waddle.

Apple Watch

If you use the Apple Watch app, your steps, walks, and your penguin's state sync between your iPhone and your watch over Apple's own connection between them. Nothing extra goes to our servers. Walks you start on the watch are saved to Apple Health on your devices.

Notifications

Notifications are optional. The app uses them for goal celebrations, gentle reminders, waddle pokes and new waddle members, Rookery roasts and cheers, and, if you start a free trial, a reminder before it ends. Most are scheduled on your device. A few come from outside it:

  • When someone joins your waddle, iCloud sends you the notification.
  • Waddle pokes and Rookery roasts and cheers are sent by our server through Apple's push service, so they arrive even when the app is closed. For this, our server stores your device's push token, your time zone offset (so it can stay quiet at night), and the random IDs of penguins you've blocked, under a random ID made on your phone. It doesn't know who is in which waddle.
  • A poke's message passes through our server on its way to your friend's phone and isn't stored.

Push tokens are deleted when you turn off Rookery notifications, leave the Rookery, leave all your waddles, or when Apple tells us a token no longer works. You can turn all notifications off anytime in iOS Settings.

A random install ID

The app makes a random ID when it's installed and sends it with requests to our server, so we can apply daily limits and stop abuse. It isn't linked to you, your Apple ID, or your device's advertising ID.

Purchases

Penguin Plus is bought through Apple, and Apple processes the payment. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history from Apple, never your payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy for details.

Who processes data for us

  • PostHog collects usage stats (unless you turn them off).
  • Cloudflare hosts our server (Gary's line server, the Rookery, and push notifications). It may keep standard request logs, such as IP addresses, for security and operations.
  • Anthropic writes Gary's fresh lines (unless you turn them off) and helps check Rookery names.
  • RevenueCat manages subscriptions.
  • Apple provides Health, iCloud, push notifications, payments, and the weather (WeatherKit).

What we don't do

  • No account, sign-up, or email collection in the app.
  • No advertising, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites. PostHog is our only analytics, and it never gets your health data.
  • We never receive your location. If you turn on the weather, your approximate location goes only to Apple Weather. PostHog sees only your country and region.
  • No selling or renting of personal information.

If you've turned on “Share with App Developers” in iOS, Apple may send us anonymous crash and usage reports. That's controlled by Apple and your iOS settings.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics, ads, or third-party scripts or fonts. Our hosting provider, DigitalOcean, may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) for security and operations.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and deleting data

  • Health access: change it anytime in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Penguin.
  • Usage stats: Settings › Share usage stats. Turning it off stops all analytics events.
  • Fresh lines from AI: Settings › Fresh lines from AI.
  • Weather: Settings › Weather in his sky, or Location in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Penguin.
  • Rookery: Settings › Show my penguin in the Rookery. Turning it off deletes your penguin from our server.
  • Reset: Settings › Reset everything clears his progress on your phone, removes your penguin from the Rookery, and gives you a new analytics ID. Your Apple Health data is untouched.
  • Waddles: leaving a waddle stops your updates, but your last summary stays with that waddle until its owner removes you or closes it. Closing your own waddle deletes it for everyone.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from your device.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. For anything not covered above, email us at gary@penguinathletics.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we'll also note it in the app or on this site.
