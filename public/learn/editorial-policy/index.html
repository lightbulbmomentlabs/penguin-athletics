Editorial policy

The penguin is a joke. The facts are not.

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Who writes these articles

Articles in Learn are published by the Penguin Athletics editorial team at Lightbulb Moment Labs, Inc., the company behind the Penguin walking app. We are not doctors. Our job is to read the research carefully and explain it plainly, with every claim traced back to its source.

How we choose sources

  • We prefer systematic reviews and meta-analyses, large long-running studies, and guidance from public health bodies such as the CDC, NIH, WHO, NHS, the American Heart Association, and the American College of Sports Medicine.
  • Every health claim and every number is cited, and the full source list sits at the end of each article.
  • We open and read every source we cite, and we only cite it for what it actually says.
  • When a study only shows a link (not cause and effect), we say so. When evidence is weak or mixed, we say that too.

How we use AI

Articles are researched and drafted with the help of AI tools. A person on our team reviews every draft, checks its sources, and approves it before it's published. Nothing goes live automatically.

Medical review

Articles that have been reviewed by a qualified health professional show “Reviewed by,” with that person's name and credentials, under the headline. Articles without that line have been checked by our editorial team only.

The voice

The penguin shows up at the start and end of each article to keep things fun. He roasts procrastination, never people. We don't make jokes about bodies, weight, health conditions, or identity, and we don't use shame to motivate anyone.

Not medical advice

Everything in Learn is general information, not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Talk to your doctor before starting a new exercise routine, especially if you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, diabetes, joint problems, or are pregnant. Never stop or change a medication without your doctor's guidance.

Updates and corrections

We review articles when new research changes the picture, and the date at the top of each article shows when it was last updated. Spotted a mistake or an outdated claim? Email hello@penguinathletics.com with the article link and what's wrong. We fix confirmed errors quickly and update the article's date.

Independence

Learn has no ads and no sponsored content. Articles may mention the Penguin app, which we make. We never let that change what the research says.

Now go touch grass. Or ice.
