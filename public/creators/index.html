Penguin is a step counter with a pudgy, extremely confident penguin who gets fitter every time you walk. We're looking for creators to show him off. Small followings very welcome.

Who we're
looking for.

  • 200–5,000 followers
  • TikTok or Instagram
  • Walking, fitness, wellness, or everyday life
  • Funny on camera, or willing to try
  • An iPhone (the app is iPhone only for now)

You don't need a big following or a ring light. You need taste, timing, and a willingness to be out-confidenced by a cartoon bird.

What you get.

  • Free Penguin PlusA full year, on us, as soon as you're in. Every expedition, every new fit.
  • $40 per videoFor each approved TikTok or Reel, up to 3 a month. Post to both and it still counts as one.

How it works

  1. Apply

    Send us your handle and one video you're proud of. Takes a minute.

  2. Get set up

    We'll send a Penguin Plus code and a short brief. Use the app for a few days so it's real.

  3. Send a draft

    Share your video before it goes up. We reply within two business days, usually with “ship it.”

  4. Post and get paid

    Post it with the paid-partnership label. We pay within 14 days of it going live.

The ground rules

  • Use the paid-partnership label or #ad. Always.
  • Actually use the app. Real steps, real reactions.
  • Roast the penguin, never people. No jokes about bodies, weight, or anyone's looks.
  • No weight-loss promises or medical claims. Walking is good for you; that's enough.
  • We can repost your video on our accounts with credit. Paid ads with your video are a separate ask, and paid separately.

Questions

Do I need a big following?

No. 200 to 5,000 followers is exactly who we're looking for. Small accounts often have the most engaged audiences.

Do I get creative control?

Yes. We'll share what the app does and what to avoid, never a script. The best videos sound like you.

Can I post on TikTok and Instagram?

Please do. Cross-posting the same video counts as one video, and views from both count toward bonuses.

What if my video doesn't get approved?

We'll tell you why and you can tweak it. If it's a hard no, you keep the free year of Penguin Plus anyway.

I'm on Android. Can I join?

Not yet. Penguin is iPhone only for now, and the videos need to show the real app.
